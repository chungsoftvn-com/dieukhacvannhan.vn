Product description

Guanyin Bodhisattva in peaceful repose is one of the most beloved statues, recreating the Bodhisattva seated in meditation, serene and at ease. Every fold of the cloud-like robes and every ornamental detail is carved meticulously by our artisans, expressing the compassion and tolerance of the Bodhisattva.

The statue is made from high-grade poly resin, durable in all weather, ideal for home worship or temple spaces. Size, paint and style can all be customized on request.
