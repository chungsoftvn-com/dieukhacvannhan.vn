About us

Van Nhan Buddha Statue Workshop

First of all, Van Nhan Buddha Statue respectfully greets Buddhists and valued customers near and far, and wishes everyone good health and prosperity in every field.

We specialize in decorative fine-art sculpture projects for temples and pagodas: ornaments, ritual objects, sacred animals and ritual symbols, outdoor statues, restoration of venerable statues, display decoration, temple park landscaping and Buddhist reliefs.

We also fabricate and supply products in many materials such as poly resin, AB resin, bronze, stone, wood, ceramic and foam, meeting every need of size, style and color.
